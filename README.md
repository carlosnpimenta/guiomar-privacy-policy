# Privacy Policy for GUIOMAR Field Electrical Tools

**Last updated: January 29, 2026**

## Overview
GUIOMAR Field Electrical Tools is an offline electrical calculator app for electricians in Ontario, Canada.

## Data Collection
This app does NOT collect, store, or share any personal data.

## How It Works
All calculations are performed locally on your device. No data is sent to any server.

## Permissions
This app does not require any special permissions or internet access to function.
